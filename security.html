Security boundary

Security is part of the model, not an isolated concern.

Railix is designed so access, secrets, production visibility and operational responsibility are explicit parts of the platform boundary rather than scattered through application code.

Governed by design

Keep control close to the model and out of accidental runtime behavior.

Role-based access

Teams can separate who builds, who deploys, who observes and who operates.

Read-only production visibility

Production access is deliberate, explicit and designed to be read-only where needed.

Encrypted environment secrets

Environment secrets are encrypted and access-controlled instead of embedded into apps.

Security model

The platform governs creation. The artifact runs with only what it needs.

Identity

Users and automation are authenticated at the platform boundary, not treated as anonymous editors of production behavior.

Authorization

Roles define who can edit models, manage environments, view production state and trigger operational actions.

Secrets

Environment secrets are encrypted and scoped to the places that need them, instead of being copied into model logic or source files.

Visibility

Production inspection is explicit, read-only by default and separate from permissions that can change or redeploy the product.

Compiled boundary

Portable does not mean uncontrolled.

A compiled Railix application should not carry editor privileges, platform admin access or unbounded production permissions just because it was created in Railix.

The goal is a narrow runtime surface: the application gets the configuration, credentials and execution paths required for its target environment, while governance remains visible in the model.
