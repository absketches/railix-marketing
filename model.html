Railix Creator

A product model humans and AI systems can reason about.

Railix Creator makes software visible and executable. Teams can see what the product does, how it is verified, where it runs, which secrets it needs and how production should be handled.

One shared model

Replace separate diagrams, YAML estates, test assumptions and operational folklore.

Developers get control. Product teams get legibility. Support gets operational context. Auditors get a traceable model. AI systems get a structured source of truth instead of guessing from scattered files.

Flows and Steps describe product behavior directly, while examples prove the expected outcomes locally before the compiled artifact is created.
